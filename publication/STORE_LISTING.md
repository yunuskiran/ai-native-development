## Description

AI can produce a convincing implementation before a team has agreed what makes it acceptable. Engineering with AI: Beyond Vibe Coding examines how engineers can keep decisions, context, and verification connected as more work moves to coding agents.

Yunus Kiran compares BMAD Method, the selected GSD Core lineage, AWS AI-DLC workflow profiles, and AI Unified Process through common engineering questions. A synthetic vendor-access approval service carries those questions through a feature, a policy change, and a tenant-boundary repair. Spec-Driven Development provides a cross-cutting lens for ownership and maintenance.

The book includes worked decision records, context handoffs, acceptance controls, diagnostic exercises, and a small TypeScript policy model with passing local checks. It distinguishes documented mechanisms from observed outcomes and gives teams a framework for choosing and evaluating a workflow under their own constraints.

Written for practicing developers, technical leads, and architects. Method accounts use dated source snapshots. The case is a qualitative walkthrough, and the book reports no four-method benchmark or production certification.
