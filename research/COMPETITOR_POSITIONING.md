## Proposed independent reader promise

Help a practicing engineer choose and adapt a process for a specific project, explain the decision rights and artifact costs, and distinguish documented mechanisms from demonstrated outcomes. The case study should let readers inspect comparable decisions across methods, including unsuccessful steps and limits. This is a proposed promise, not a finding that any method works better.

| Risk of overlap | Proposed control | Review evidence |
|---|---|---|
| AIUP's narrative becomes the book's default structure | Shared comparison questions precede method chapters; AIUP has the same evidence obligations as the other three. | Chapter briefs identify unique questions; no method defines the scoring criteria alone. |
| Tutorial sequence resembles existing spec-to-code instruction | Organize synthesis around choosing, changing, repairing and operating software. Keep installation recipes in a versioned appendix. | Cross-reference map gives each concept one home; M1 checks the actual outline. |
| Case study is another generic task manager | Consider tenant-scoped vendor-access approvals with authorization boundaries, audit history and a policy-change episode. | Requirements and fixtures originate in this repository; author approves domain; no competitor sample is used as the scaffold. |
| Examples, prompts or diagrams inherit outside expression | Create original artifacts from a neutral business brief; attribute concepts and clear any actual reuse. | Per-asset provenance, citation and permissions register before drafting/release. |
| Evidence reads like a maintainer endorsement | Separate source-described design, observed behavior, field reports and our interpretation. | Claim register, failed-run retention and independent technical review. |
| “Beyond Vibe Coding” appears to identify an existing book | Keep author's working title now; review subtitle/cover/catalog differentiation later. | D12 records the author's packaging choice; no unilateral rename. |

Distinctiveness depends on execution: equal scrutiny, original requirements, transparent versioning and evidence readers can inspect. It cannot be certified from a title or a proposed TOC.
