## Reader question

Which parts of the engineering decision system remain useful as models, tools and process frameworks change?

## Reader outcomes

- Distinguish present evidence from forecast and identify the assumptions in a future scenario.
- Specify triggers for reevaluating a versioned process choice.
- Carry the book's decision/evidence procedure forward without depending on a fixed product catalog.

## Scope and dependencies

Own scenarios, open research questions and reevaluation. Exclude release predictions, deterministic capability timelines and a fresh uncited tool survey.

Prerequisites: C01 historical caution, C09 evidence limits, C10 decision update rules and C11–C13 bounded observations.

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will close the book by separating dated observations, unresolved research questions and possible future scenarios. It will consider how changing model capabilities, workflow enforcement and artifact standards could affect the decisions readers learned to make, without forecasting a guaranteed path. The comparison unit and evidence rules will become a practical reevaluation checklist: what changed, which assumptions broke and what must be tested again. The ending will return to accountable judgment and proportionate process, giving readers a way to continue learning while retaining the limits of the edition's evidence.
