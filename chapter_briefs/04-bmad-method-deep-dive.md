## Reader question

What decisions, artifacts and review obligations does a selected BMAD profile introduce for the common increment?

## Reader outcomes

- Map a selected BMAD profile's decisions, artifacts and boundaries.
- Distinguish core behavior, optional modules and host-dependent behavior.
- Evaluate a small/change variant without inferring effectiveness from documentation alone.

## Scope and dependencies

Own BMAD's selected profile and native artifact trail. Exclude repeated general taxonomy, exhaustive module installation and unsupported advantages over the other methods.

Prerequisites: C03 common vocabulary/increment; M2 BMAD dossier and chosen source/profile before method-specific prose.

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will examine a defined BMAD source line and implementation profile, making its scope and optional modules explicit before explaining the workflow. It will follow the common business increment through planning choices, specialized perspectives, artifact handoffs and verification, recording what people must decide at each point. A small-change variant and an existing-code variant will test whether the selected process can be reduced without misrepresenting it. The chapter will distinguish documented design from actual observations and close with evidence gaps and conditional fit rather than a promotional verdict.
