## Reader question

How does a selected AIUP profile connect owned requirements and use cases to implementation, tests and later changes?

## Reader outcomes

- Trace an AIUP artifact chain with owners, review points and unresolved links visible.
- Distinguish the method from plugins, stack adapters and the Studio product.
- Evaluate persistent specifications and change handling without equating traceability assertions with proof.

## Scope and dependencies

Own the chosen AIUP profile and bounded use-case trail. Exclude a full use-case-writing handbook, the existing book's sample application and untested plugin compatibility claims.

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will follow AIUP's requirements and use-case artifacts for the same neutral SaaS increment used by the other methods. It will examine ownership, review, traceability and change propagation, distinguishing the method's core from construction plugins and products. The TypeScript/Node.js preference will be treated as a compatibility question for a selected adapter rather than proof that every documented workflow is portable. The chapter will give AIUP the same evidence obligations and limitations as its peers, using original case material and avoiding a condensed recreation of the existing AIUP publication.
