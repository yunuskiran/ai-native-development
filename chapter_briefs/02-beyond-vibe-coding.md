## Reader question

What makes an AI-assisted change defensible engineering work, and when is a lightweight approach sufficient?

## Reader outcomes

- Recognize missing decisions and acceptance evidence without labeling all conversational coding irresponsible.
- Choose a proportionate minimum record for a small change.
- Explain the thesis as an argument to test rather than a universal productivity law.

## Scope and dependencies

Own engineering discipline, accountability and proportionate process. Exclude the historical timeline, method rankings, installation recipes and C13's full diagnostic catalog.

Prerequisites: C01's bounded historical frame; existing knowledge of ordinary code review and tests.

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will define the engineering problem behind the title using observable decisions, preserved context and acceptance evidence. It will follow a small change from a conversational request through clarification, implementation and review, separating useful exploration from unsupported acceptance. A counterexample will show why a tiny repair may need only a compact intent, a constrained diff and meaningful checks. The chapter will give readers a vocabulary for proportionate discipline without dismissing informal workflows or equating more documentation with better work. Detailed method classification and the later failure catalog remain separate.
