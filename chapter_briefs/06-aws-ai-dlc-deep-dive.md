## Proposed abstract

This chapter will separate the original AI-DLC concept from a specific versioned implementation and profile before mapping phases, collaboration and approvals. It will follow the same SaaS increment, identifying where business and technical decisions are proposed, reviewed and retained. Differences in profile scope and ceremony will be treated as part of the intervention rather than interchangeable labels. The chapter will examine what a local solo example can demonstrate and which team/operations claims require external observation. It will close with a qualified profile assessment, keeping vendor outcome language distinct from measured evidence.
