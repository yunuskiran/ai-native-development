## Reader question

How can readers compare AI-driven development processes without confusing a methodology, an approach and an execution tool?

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will build the common vocabulary required by the four deep dives. It will distinguish named process frameworks, cross-cutting specification practices and implementation hosts while acknowledging that a toolkit can contain substantial process guidance. The common SaaS business problem and a bounded increment will be introduced without revealing a preferred method's artifact structure. Readers will learn the comparison dimensions, evidence categories and version/profile unit before encountering individual methods. A short SDD primer will supply prerequisites; chapter 8 will own the complete specification-lifecycle analysis and chapter 11 the full case account.
