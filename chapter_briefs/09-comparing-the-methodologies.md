# 9. Comparing the Methodologies

Status: **M1 ARCHITECTURE BRIEF — NOT MANUSCRIPT**. Author-supplied title/order preserved. Chapter ID: C09. Approved planning allocation: **5,000 words**. M1 architecture accepted by the author on 2026-10-31; factual support still requires M2 evidence gates.

## Reader question

What can the available evidence actually establish about the selected methods' mechanisms and tradeoffs?

## Reader outcomes

- Read evidence-qualified comparison tables without confusing documentation coverage with effectiveness.
- Explain tradeoffs using the same task and explicit profile/version conditions.
- Identify when comparison is impossible or must be downgraded to a walkthrough.

## Scope and dependencies

Own evidence synthesis and comparable tradeoffs. Exclude a universal winner, arbitrary weighted scores and C10's scenario recommendations.

Prerequisites: C03 instrument; C04–C08 qualified accounts; M2 dossiers and any actual M4 records. C11 is a forward reference for the complete case evidence, not a hidden prerequisite to definitions.

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will bring the four profile-specific accounts onto the common comparison instrument. It will distinguish required mechanisms, optional modules and host dependencies before considering any observed outcomes. Readers will see how to read evidence gaps, conflicting descriptions and version changes without converting missing information into a zero score. The shared business increment will anchor artifact and decision comparisons; any case outcomes will be linked to actual records in chapter 11. Tables will remain explicitly unassessed where evidence is insufficient, and scenario recommendations will be deferred to chapter 10.
