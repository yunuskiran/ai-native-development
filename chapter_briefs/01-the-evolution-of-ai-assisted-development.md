# 1. The Evolution of AI-Assisted Development

Status: **M1 ARCHITECTURE BRIEF — NOT MANUSCRIPT**. Author-supplied title/order preserved. Chapter ID: C01. Approved planning allocation: **3,000 words**. M1 architecture accepted by the author on 2026-10-31; factual support still requires M2 evidence gates.

## Reader question

How has AI participation changed software development work, and which engineering responsibilities remain with people?

## Reader outcomes

- Distinguish capability evolution from an unsupported claim that an engineering responsibility disappeared.
- Identify the evidence needed for a historical timeline and a claim about adoption.
- Explain the book's central question without assuming that all tasks need a heavy process.

## Scope and dependencies

Own the historical frame and responsibilities. Exclude product installation surveys, detailed SDD taxonomy, a complete history of software engineering and predictions about future model capability.

Prerequisites: No prior chapter. Ordinary experience with source control, tests and code review is assumed.

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will establish a bounded historical frame for the book, tracing changes in assistance, delegated work and workflow coordination through dated primary evidence. It will distinguish changes in capability from changes in responsibility, showing why generated implementation still needs requirements, review and operational ownership. A small maintenance task will introduce the difference between producing code and establishing that a change is acceptable. The chapter will end with the research questions that motivate the book, leaving process taxonomy and the practical critique of informal development to the next two chapters.
