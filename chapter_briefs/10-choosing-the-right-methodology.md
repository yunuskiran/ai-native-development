# 10. Choosing the Right Methodology

Status: **M1 ARCHITECTURE BRIEF — NOT MANUSCRIPT**. Author-supplied title/order preserved. Chapter ID: C10. Approved planning allocation: **4,500 words**. M1 architecture accepted by the author on 2026-10-31; factual support still requires M2 evidence gates.

## Reader question

How can a team choose and adapt a development process for explicit constraints without pretending there is one best method?

## Reader outcomes

- Write a conditional selection record with constraints, candidates, evidence and uncertainty.
- Recognize when lightweight work or a narrow practice is preferable to full-method adoption.
- Define a reversible evaluation and stop/exit criteria without promising unmeasured benefit.

## Scope and dependencies

Own scenario-based choice and adaptation. Exclude reprinting C09 tables, guaranteed compliance/productivity and unexecuted case outcomes.

Prerequisites: C03 vocabulary and C09 evidence synthesis; C11 is a detailed example reference and C12 a forward reference for assurance depth.

## Proposed abstract

This chapter will turn the preceding comparison into a reproducible decision procedure. It will begin with project conditions and non-negotiable constraints, then filter candidate profiles using evidence and uncertainty rather than popularity. Scenarios will include a tiny repair, an uncertain product increment, an existing application and work requiring stronger review duties. Each decision will state assumptions, adoption costs, missing evidence and exit conditions. A reversible pilot will be proposed as a learning step rather than proof of long-term fit, and any combination of practices will preserve clear ownership and traceability.
