## Per-chapter acceptance

1. Brief: one question, reader outcomes, scope, dependencies and distinct purpose accepted.
2. Research: factual claims mapped to approved sources, locators and exact profiles; unverified claims remain visible.
3. Outline: 5–8 major sections with a narrative/example role; prerequisites resolved before full treatment.
4. Draft: original prose within scope, citations and provisional word allocation; no tool-as-method confusion.
5. Technical review: exact terminology, runnable artifacts, security/tenant boundaries and test results checked where relevant. Illustrative code is labeled and cannot count as runnable evidence.
6. Editorial review: remove repetition and promotional language; apply style, cross-references and accessibility rules.
7. Gate: author/reviewer disposition recorded, with unresolved issues and no silent advancement.

## Evidence rules that can block later acceptance

- Unsupported effectiveness, superiority, enterprise suitability, security or production claims cannot be accepted as fact. Attribute and qualify claims or leave the outcome Not assessed.
- Passing tests generated alongside implementation is insufficient as an independent acceptance oracle. Record actual commands/results and negative/boundary checks for executable material.
- A working local demonstration does not validate team collaboration, regulatory compliance or production operation. Claims must match observation scope.
- Method names, core modules, optional add-ons, profiles and host/model dependencies must remain distinct. Compare the same bounded business work, not unequal tutorials.
- Citation range bookkeeping is not semantic support validation. The Nov 2 BMAD locator correction prompts a wider passage-to-claim audit; unavailable fresh source retrievals remain disclosed rather than counted as successful inspections.
- No copied external prompt collections, book content or figures enter publication without source/rights review. Independent wording alone does not establish complete originality.
- Missing evidence, failed/aborted runs and protocol deviations remain visible; do not invent or selectively hide results.

## Independent review and records

Assign named people and disclose relevant method/vendor/community interests. AI role passes can assist research and editing but are not independent human review. A gate record identifies artifact versions, reviewed scope, approver, date, unresolved issues and accepted exceptions.

M1 review should decide audience/length/domain and architecture readiness. Stack/model/profile freeze, paid resources, case execution, prose and publication remain later decisions; do not block architecture preparation merely because those later validations are still pending.
